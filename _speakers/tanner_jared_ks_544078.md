talk: "Deep neural network initialisation: Nonlinear activations impact on the Gaussian process"
abstract: "Randomly initialised deep neural networks are known to generate a Gaussian process for their pre-activation intermediate layers. We will review this line of research with extensions to deep networks having structured random entries such as block-sparse or low-rank weight matrices. We will then discuss how the choice of nonlinear activations impacts the evolution of the Gaussian process. Specifically we will discuss why sparsifying nonlinear activations such as soft thresholding are unstable, we will show conditions to overcome such issues, and we will show how increasing the variance of the hidden layer Gaussian process can improve training instability for sparsifying activations. This work is joint with Ilan Price (DeepMind), and from Oxford: Alireza Naderi, Thiziri Nait Saada, and Emily Dent."
