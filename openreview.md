# CPAL OpenReview

CPAL 2027 uses **OpenReview** to host submissions, reviews, author responses, and discussion. Proceedings submissions are reviewed double-blind; Recent Spotlight submissions are reviewed single-blind.

See the [Submission Tracks]({{ site.baseurl }}/tracks/) page for submission and review policies and the [Key Dates]({{ site.baseurl }}/deadlines/) page for the review calendar.
