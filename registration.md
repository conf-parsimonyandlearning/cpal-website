# Registration

CPAL 2027 is an **in-person conference** taking place **March 23–26, 2027** at **Hitotsubashi Hall in Tokyo, Japan**.

At least one author of each accepted Proceedings or Recent Spotlight contribution must register for CPAL 2027 and present the work in person. Venue and transportation information is available on the [Logistics and Venue]({{ site.baseurl }}/venue/) page.
